# Project-Proposal-CS2---Water-Electricity-Bill-Calculator
#Water & Electricity Bill Calculator

•Problem Statement
-Some home’s may forget their total bill’s or over-use and waste the resource’s of their providers.

•Project Objective
-Help home’s observe their bill costs and help facilities discourage over-usage of resources.

•Planned Features
-It calculates your water bill based off which company you are billed from (ex: Prime Water $4/liter, Manila Water $3/liter, or S-Water Service $2/liter) Then the electricity as well depending on the providers rate (ex: Leyeco $4/kwh, or Flash $2/kwh). Then displays its total bill for you to pay.

•Planned inputs and outputs
-Inputs: Pervious Water & Electric meter reading, Present meter reading. Billing company name for both resources.
-Output: “Hello customer, your water bill from [Water_Company_Name] is $[Water Bill]. And the electric bill from [Electricity_Name] would be $[Electric Bill]”
